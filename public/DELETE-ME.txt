The PWA manifest used to live here as a static file. It is now generated from
your BRAND settings in src/lib/config.ts by src/app/manifest.ts, so renaming
your product also renames the home-screen icon. It is still served at the same
URL: /manifest.webmanifest

(The sandbox this was built in could not delete files, only rename them — hence
this note instead of a clean removal.)
